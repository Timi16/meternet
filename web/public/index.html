A taxi meter for data.

流量计价器 · 付多少，用多少

Your browser runs its own Fiber node and pays for every 2 MB as it arrives. Keep paying and the data keeps flowing. Stop, and the meter cuts you off within 15 seconds. Every payment hash lands on the public board.
